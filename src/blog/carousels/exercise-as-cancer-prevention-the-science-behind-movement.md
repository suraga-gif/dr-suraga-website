  Can Exercise Actually Prevent Cancer? 🏃‍♂️🛡️

  The answer is a scientifically-backed YES.

  Many people view exercise only as a tool for weight loss, but it is actually
  one of the most powerful “medicines” we have for cancer prevention.

  A massive study published in JAMA Internal Medicine—reviewing data from 1.44
  million adults—found that regular physical activity is linked to a
  significantly lower risk of 13 different types of cancer.

  What the data shows:

  Higher activity levels led to incredible drops in risk, including:

  • 42% lower risk for Esophageal cancer

  • 27% lower risk for Liver cancer

  • 26% lower risk for Lung cancer

  The most important takeaway?

  This protection isn’t just about the number on the weighing scale. Even if
  your BMI stays the same, exercise works on a cellular level to lower internal
  inflammation and balance hormones that could otherwise trigger cancer growth.

  Your Weekly Goal:

  To get these protective benefits, health guidelines recommend:

  • 150 MINUTES PER WEEK of moderate activity (like brisk walking).

  • OR 75 MINUTES PER WEEK of vigorous activity (like running or swimming).
