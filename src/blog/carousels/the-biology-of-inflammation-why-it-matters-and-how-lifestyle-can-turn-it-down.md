caption: >-
  Inflammation is often misunderstood. 🛑

  It’s often blamed for everything—from cancer to heart disease. But the truth
  is more nuanced.

  Inflammation is not the enemy. 🛡️

  It’s your body’s natural defense system—essential for healing injuries and
  fighting infections.

  The concern arises when inflammation doesn’t switch off. ⏳

  While acute inflammation is short-term and protective, chronic inflammation is
  low-grade, persistent, and often silent.

  Over time, this can disrupt normal cell repair, damage DNA, and create an
  environment where disease can develop.

  🌱 Think of it this way:

  It acts less like the cause (the seed), and more like the fertile soil that
  supports disease progression.

  The good news? It’s modifiable. ✅

  You can help turn down the dial through:

  • Regular physical activity 🏃‍♂️

  • Balanced nutrition 🥗

  • Quality sleep 😴

  • Stress control 🧘‍♂️

  Understanding inflammation isn’t about fear—it’s about biology. And knowledge
  is a powerful tool for long-term health.
