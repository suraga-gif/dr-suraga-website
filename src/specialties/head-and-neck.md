## Which cancers are included?

- **Mouth (oral cavity):** tongue, cheek lining, gums, floor of the mouth and palate
- **Throat (pharynx) and voice box (larynx)**
- **Salivary glands**, including the parotid gland in front of the ear
- **Thyroid gland**, at the front of the neck

## Warning signs

See a doctor if any of these last more than two to three weeks:

- A mouth ulcer that does not heal
- A white or red patch in the mouth
- A lump in the neck
- Difficulty or pain when swallowing
- A hoarse voice
- A swelling in front of the ear or below the jaw
- A lump in the front of the neck that moves when you swallow (thyroid)
- Difficulty opening the mouth fully, or teeth that become loose for no reason

## Risk factors

The biggest risk is **tobacco in any form**: gutka, khaini, betel quid with tobacco, cigarettes and beedis. Areca nut and alcohol add to the risk. Stopping at any age lowers it.

## How it is diagnosed

- Examination of the mouth, throat and neck, sometimes with a small camera (endoscopy)
- A biopsy of the suspicious area, or a fine needle test (FNAC) for thyroid and salivary gland lumps
- Ultrasound of the neck for thyroid lumps
- CT or MRI scans, and sometimes a PET-CT scan

## Treatment

- **Surgery** removes the tumour with a margin of healthy tissue, usually together with a **neck dissection** to remove lymph nodes.
- **Reconstruction** using tissue from elsewhere in the body restores form and function when a large area is removed.
- **Radiotherapy, sometimes with chemotherapy,** is given after surgery when needed, or as the main treatment for some throat cancers.
- **Thyroid cancer** is treated by removing part or all of the thyroid gland (thyroidectomy), sometimes with the lymph nodes, and in some cases radioactive iodine afterwards.

## Scar-sparing robotic surgery

Conventional thyroid and neck operations leave a visible scar on the front or side of the neck. For selected patients, robotic surgery moves the cuts to places that clothing or the natural folds of the body hide:

- **Robotic thyroidectomy** removes the thyroid gland without a cut on the front of the neck
- [RIA-MIND](/robotic-surgery/ria-mind/) for neck dissection, through small cuts below the collarbone
- [PRIA](/robotic-surgery/pria/) for parotid gland tumours, through the same route

## Recovery

- Speech and swallowing therapy, dental care, and stopping tobacco are all part of recovery.
- Follow-up visits are frequent in the first two years.
