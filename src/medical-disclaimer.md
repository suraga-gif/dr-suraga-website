## Information, not medical advice

The articles, videos and pages on this website explain cancer and its treatment in general terms. They are written to help you understand a condition and prepare questions for your doctor.

They are **not** a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a substitute for a consultation. Every patient is different. Decisions about your care should be made with a doctor who has examined you and seen your reports.

## No doctor–patient relationship through the website

Reading this website, sending an appointment request, or sending a message on WhatsApp or by email does not create a doctor–patient relationship. That relationship begins at your consultation.

Please do not send detailed medical records or ask for a diagnosis through the website. Bring your reports to the clinic, where they can be reviewed properly.

## Emergencies

This website and its contact options are not monitored for emergencies. **If you have a medical emergency, go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.**

## Treatment outcomes

Descriptions of operations and techniques, including robotic surgery, explain what is possible for suitable patients. They are not a promise of any particular result. Whether a treatment is right for you depends on the type and stage of your cancer and on your general health.

## Accuracy and links

Information on this website is reviewed by Dr. Suraga Belakawadi and is believed to be accurate when published. Medical knowledge changes, and pages may not reflect the latest research. Links to other websites and research papers are provided for reference, and their content is the responsibility of their publishers.
