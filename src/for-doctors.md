## What to send

To help give a useful opinion quickly, please share:

1. A short clinical summary
2. Imaging: CT, MRI or PET-CT reports, and the images if available
3. Biopsy, FNAC or histopathology reports
4. Details of any treatment already given

Please remove identifying details, or share them only with the patient's consent.

## Cases Dr. Suraga takes on

- Upper GI cancers: oesophagus and stomach
- Breast cancer
- Colorectal cancer, including sphincter-preserving surgery for rectal cancer
- Gynaecological cancers, including cytoreductive surgery and HIPEC
- Pancreato-biliary cancers
- Head and neck cancers, including robotic thyroidectomy, robotic scar-sparing neck dissection ([RIA-MIND](/robotic-surgery/ria-mind/)) and parotidectomy ([PRIA](/robotic-surgery/pria/))
- Soft tissue sarcomas and skin cancers
- Second opinions on surgical options

Referring doctors receive an update on the treatment plan and on the outcome of surgery, so that care continues smoothly when the patient returns to you.
