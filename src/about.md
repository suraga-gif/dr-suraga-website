Dr. Suraga Belakawadi is a surgical oncologist, a surgeon who specialises in removing cancer. His practice covers cancers of the oesophagus and stomach, breast, colon and rectum, the female reproductive organs, the pancreas and bile ducts, the head and neck, and soft tissue sarcomas. Wherever it is the right choice for the patient, he uses robotic and laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery.

## Qualifications and training

1. **MBBS**
2. **DNB, General Surgery**
3. **MCh, Surgical Oncology**, awarded with a gold medal
4. **Fellowship in Robotic and Laparoscopic Surgical Oncology**
5. **FMASO**

## Current position

Attending Consultant, Department of Surgical Oncology, KIMS-MACS Hospital, Mahadevapura, Bengaluru.

## Areas of special interest

- Robotic surgery for head and neck cancers, including scar-sparing neck dissection ([RIA-MIND](/robotic-surgery/ria-mind/)) and parotid surgery ([PRIA](/robotic-surgery/pria/))
- Rectal cancer surgery that preserves the sphincter, and quality of life afterwards

## Research

Dr. Suraga's published work includes:

- **Low anterior resection syndrome after sphincter-preserving surgery for rectal cancer**, a study of predictive factors and patients' perceived functional satisfaction (*Discover Oncology*, 2026), as lead author
- **Robotic infraclavicular approach for minimally invasive neck dissection**, an evaluation of safety, oncologic efficacy and the learning curve in 62 patients (*Frontiers in Oncology*, 2026), as co-author
- **Comparison of MRI with surgical and pathological features in carcinoma rectum** (*Indian Journal of Surgical Oncology*, 2024), as co-author
