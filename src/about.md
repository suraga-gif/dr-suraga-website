Dr. Suraga Belakawadi is a surgical oncologist, a surgeon who specialises in removing cancer. His practice covers cancers of the oesophagus and stomach, breast, colon and rectum, the female reproductive organs, the pancreas and bile ducts, the head and neck including the thyroid, the skin, and soft tissue sarcomas. Wherever it is the right choice for the patient, he uses robotic and laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery.

He believes good cancer care starts with listening. He takes time to explain each option clearly, involves families in decisions, and plans every treatment around the person, not only the disease.

## Qualifications and training

- **FMASO: Fellowship in Robotic and Laparoscopic Surgical Oncology**, 2024 to 2025: Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences
- **MCh, Surgical Oncology**, 2020 to 2023: Acharya Harihar Post Graduate Institute of Cancer, Cuttack, Odisha. **Gold medalist.**
- **DNB, General Surgery**, 2016 to 2019: Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Government Medical College and CPR Hospital, Kolhapur
- **MBBS**, 2009 to 2014: Al-Ameen Medical College, Vijayapura (Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences)

## Current position

Attending Consultant, Department of Surgical Oncology, KIMS-MACS Hospital, Mahadevpura, Bengaluru.
